A one-line family scene for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2): a six-year-old tapes her crayon drawing of the family to the fridge and whispers, "I drew us happy, so we stay happy" — and Dad has been grinning at the fridge ever since.

Why it makes the judge proud: it is one small, specific domestic moment — a crooked-smile crayon family on the fridge — that is warm, gently funny, and heartfelt without ever saying so. A child trying to keep her family happy with a drawing is the kind of love a proud judge smiles at. The whole scene fits in a single breath, no word wasted.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the scene to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
